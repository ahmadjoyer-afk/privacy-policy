English Version Introduction About This Policy Reverse Keyboard is an Arabic keyboard application for Android devices. We respect and are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and what we do not collect. Data What We Do Not Collect We do not collect any text you type on the keyboard. We do not store passwords or sensitive data. We do not send your personal data to any third party. We do not use any tracking tools or user analytics. Translation Text Translation Feature When you tap the translate button in the toolbar, the text you select is sent to free, open-source translation services. The app tries the following services in order until one succeeds: MyMemory — api.mymemory.translated.net LibreTranslate — libretranslate.com and translate.argosopentech.com Apertium — apertium.org These services have their own independent privacy policies. No text is sent without an explicit action from you. Voice Voice Translation Feature When the "Voice Translation" feature is enabled and you tap the microphone button, your speech is processed locally via Android's speech recognition system. If the app detects that the spoken language differs from the keyboard language, the recognised text is sent to one of the translation services above. You can disable this feature from the app settings → Voice Translation → OFF. When disabled, speech is typed directly without any data being sent to external servers. Permissions Microphone Permission The microphone is used only when you tap the voice input button. Audio is not recorded or stored — it is processed directly by your device's speech recognition service (Google Speech). Permissions Photos & Video Permission Used to allow you to select an image or video to set as the keyboard background. Files remain on your device and are not uploaded to any server. Privacy Password Fields When typing in password fields, the app automatically stops displaying suggestions or storing any text. Clipboard Clipboard The app monitors the clipboard to display a quick-paste button in the toolbar. The last copied text is stored locally on your device only and is not sent to any external server. The clipboard is only accessed when the keyboard is open. Device Identifier Device Identifier (ANDROID_ID) The app uses the device identifier to verify user permissions within the app. This identifier is never sent to or stored on any external server. Screen OCR Screen Translation Feature When you tap the screen translation icon in the toolbar, the app requests Android's official screen capture permission (MediaProjection) via a system dialog shown to you. A persistent notification with a stop button is displayed throughout the entire session — the session never starts without a visible notification. A screen frame is captured only on your explicit request; no automatic capturing occurs. Text is analysed locally on your device using the Tesseract OCR library — no internet connection required. No screen content is ever sent to any external server. The session ends immediately when you tap the stop button in the notification or from within the app. The SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission (draw over other apps) is used solely to display the translation result as a floating overlay — it is not used for any other purpose. Advertising Advertising (AdMob) The free version displays ads via Google AdMob. This service may collect device data to show relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads from: Settings → Google → Ads. Contact Contact Us If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us through the app's page on Google Play.
