الترجمة

ميزة الترجمة النصية

عند طلبك ترجمة نصٍّ من شريط الأدوات، يُترجَم على جهازك باستخدام Google ML Kit متى أمكن، ولا يُرسل شيء حينها. وقد تُنزَّل ملفات اللغات اللازمة من Google في المرة الأولى.

وفي غير ذلك يُرسل النص الذي طلبت ترجمته فقط، مع لغة المصدر ولغة الهدف، إلى خدمات ترجمة عبر الإنترنت. وللحصول على النتيجة بسرعة، قد يُرسل إلى أكثر من خدمة في الوقت نفسه:

  • Google Translate (Google)
  • Lingva — lingva.ml وtranslate.plausibility.cloud وlingva.tiekoetter.com، وهي تمرّر النص إلى Google Translate
  • MyMemory (Translated) — api.mymemory.translated.net
  • LibreTranslate — libretranslate.com وtranslate.argosopentech.com
  • Apertium — apertium.org

بعض هذه الخدمات مستقلّة أو يديرها متطوّعون، ولكلٍّ منها شروطها وسياسة خصوصيتها، ولا يربطنا بها أي عقد. لا يُرسل أي نص دون إجراء صريح منك. ونرجو ألّا تترجم عبر الإنترنت معلومات حسّاسة، مثل كلمات المرور أو البيانات الصحية أو المالية أو بيانات الهوية.

ترجمة الشاشة

ترجمة الشاشة ونسخ نصّها

لا تعمل هاتان الميزتان إلا حين تبدأهما بنفسك. يطلب التطبيق حينها إذن التقاط الشاشة الرسمي في Android (MediaProjection) عبر نافذة النظام، ويعرض Android إشعاراً ظاهراً طوال مدة الجلسة.

  • تُقرأ صورة الشاشة على جهازك باستخدام Google ML Kit وTesseract، ولا تُرفع إلى أي خادم.
  • قد تنزّل خدمات Google Play على جهازك نماذج التعرّف على النص.
  • لا يُترجم إلا النص المُعرَّف، بالطريقة الموضّحة في قسم الترجمة النصية.
  • تنتهي الجلسة فور إيقافها من الإشعار أو من داخل التطبيق.

يُستخدم إذن العرض فوق التطبيقات الأخرى لإظهار الأيقونات والنوافذ العائمة (مثل نتيجة الترجمة والمساعد الذكي) فقط، ولا يُستخدم لأي غرض آخر.

  • المساعد الذكي: يفتح الموقع الرسمي لخدمة الذكاء الاصطناعي التي تختارها داخل نافذة في التطبيق: ChatGPT (OpenAI) أو Gemini (Google) أو Claude (Anthropic) أو Copilot (Microsoft) أو DeepSeek. تسجّل الدخول في ذلك الموقع بحسابك الخاص، وما ترسله هناك يصل مباشرةً إلى تلك الخدمة وتعالجه وفق شروطها وسياسة خصوصيتها. لا نتلقّى كلمة مرورك ولا محادثاتك، وتُحفظ ملفات تعريف الارتباط الخاصة بتسجيل الدخول على جهازك.
  • الرد التلقائي: عند نسخك نصاً، يفتح المساعد وفيه ذلك النص في خانة الرسالة. ولا يُرسل شيء حتى تضغط أنت على زر الإرسال.

لسنا تابعين لأي من هذه الخدمات، وتُذكر أسماؤها فقط لبيان ما يعمل التطبيق معه.

الإعلانات

الإعلانات (AdMob) — النسخة المجانية فقط

تعرض النسخة المجانية إعلانات من Google AdMob: إعلانات بملء الشاشة تظهر أحياناً عند التنقّل بين الصفحات أو بعد عدد من عمليات العكس، وإعلانات بمكافأة تختار مشاهدتها بنفسك لفتح بعض الميزات لمدة محدودة.

  • الموافقة: في المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية والمملكة المتحدة وسويسرا، يُعرض نموذج الموافقة من Google قبل طلب أي إعلان، ولا تبدأ خدمة الإعلانات قبل توفّر قرار الموافقة. يمكنك تغيير اختيارك لاحقاً من الصفحة 1 ← خيارات الخصوصية ← خيارات الإعلانات، حيث ينطبق ذلك.
  • ما يجمعه AdMob: المعرّف الإعلاني لجهازك، وعنوان IP، ومعلومات الجهاز، وتفاعلك مع الإعلانات. تستخدمها Google لعرض الإعلانات وتحديد تكرارها وقياسها ومنع الاحتيال، ولتخصيص الإعلانات فقط حيث توافق على ذلك. راجع كيف تستخدم Google المعلومات من التطبيقات التي تستخدم خدماتها وسياسة خصوصية Google.
  • تحكّمك: يمكنك إعادة ضبط المعرّف الإعلاني أو حذفه من إعدادات جهازك (Google ← الإعلانات).
  • الأساس القانوني: موافقتك حيث يشترطها القانون (المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية والمملكة المتحدة وسويسرا)، وفي غير ذلك مصلحتنا المشروعة في تمويل النسخة المجانية.

Introduction

About This Policy

This policy explains how “KeySnatcher: Arabic Keyboard”, published on Google Play and shown in the app as “Khatef Keyboard” (خاطف كيبورد), handles your data. The app is a keyboard for Android devices. It is available in two versions, and this policy applies to both:

  • Free version: com.khattef.keyboard.free
  • Pro version: com.khattef.keyboard

Developer and data controller: احمد وقطع. Privacy contact: ahmadjoyer@gmail.com.

Everything in this policy applies to both versions unless a section states that it applies to only one of them.

Languages

Supported Languages

The app is designed primarily for Arabic and English. These are the only two languages the app officially supports. The app interface, the built-in keyboard layouts, the help content and this policy are provided in these two languages.

Other languages may be available in certain features or through external services, such as translation, voice input, screen text recognition, or keyboard layouts that you import yourself. These languages are not officially supported. Their availability and quality may vary, and full compatibility is not guaranteed. The availability of a language in a particular feature does not mean that the app as a whole officially supports that language.

When you use another language in a feature that relies on an external service, your data is handled as described for that feature in this policy.

Summary

In Short

  • What you type is processed on your device. We do not run servers that receive or store your text, and we do not sell your data.
  • Text leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs the internet: online translation, the AI Assistant or PC Bridge.
  • Usage statistics are off by default and run only if you switch them on.
  • The Free version shows ads from Google AdMob. The Pro version has no ads.
  • There is no account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
Data

Data That Stays on Your Device

  • What you type, word suggestions, spell-check and undo history.
  • Your shortcuts, settings and themes.
  • Clipboard items and clipboard vaults.
  • Read-aloud, which uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device.

This data is never uploaded to any server. Automatic backup of app data is disabled, so it is not copied to your cloud account or transferred to another device.

Translation

Text Translation Feature

When you ask for a translation from the toolbar, the text is translated on your device with Google ML Kit whenever possible, and nothing is sent. The required language files may be downloaded from Google the first time.

Otherwise, only the text you ask to translate, together with the source and target languages, is sent to online translation services. To return a result quickly, it may be sent to more than one service at the same time:

  • Google Translate (Google)
  • Lingva — lingva.ml, translate.plausibility.cloud and lingva.tiekoetter.com, which pass the text on to Google Translate
  • MyMemory (Translated) — api.mymemory.translated.net
  • LibreTranslate — libretranslate.com and translate.argosopentech.com
  • Apertium — apertium.org

Some of these services are independent or volunteer-run. Each one has its own terms and privacy policy, and we have no contract with them. No text is sent without an explicit action from you. Please do not translate sensitive information online, such as passwords or health, financial or identity details.

Voice

Voice Translation Feature

When “Voice Translation” is turned on and you tap the microphone button, your speech is converted to text by your device’s speech-recognition service. The recognised text is then translated as described in the Text Translation section.

You can turn this feature off in the app’s settings at any time. When it is off, your speech is typed directly without translation.

Permissions

Microphone Permission

The microphone is used only after you tap the voice-input button. Your speech is converted to text by your device’s speech-recognition service (for example, Google’s), which may process it on its own servers under its own privacy policy. The app does not record or store audio.

Backgrounds

Photos & Videos

You can choose a picture or video as the keyboard background through the Android system picker, so the app can access only the file you select. Files remain on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

Privacy

Password Fields

When you type in a password field, the keyboard shows no suggestions and saves no text.

Clipboard

Clipboard

The keyboard reads the clipboard only while it is open, to offer quick paste in the toolbar. Clipboard items and clipboard vaults are stored on your device only and are not sent to any external server.

Screen OCR

Screen Translation and Screen Copy

These features run only when you start them. The app then requests Android’s official screen-capture permission (MediaProjection) through a system dialog, and Android shows a visible notification for the whole session.

  • The screen image is read on your device using Google ML Kit and Tesseract, and is not uploaded to any server.
  • Google Play services on your device may download the text-recognition models.
  • Only the recognised text is translated, as described in the Text Translation section.
  • The session ends as soon as you stop it from the notification or from within the app.

The display over other apps permission is used only to show floating icons and windows (such as translation results and the AI Assistant), and for no other purpose.

AI

AI Assistant and Auto Reply (Beta)

These features are in beta and may not always work as expected. They work only if you turn them on.

  • AI Assistant: opens the official website of the AI service you choose inside an in-app window: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), Claude (Anthropic), Copilot (Microsoft) or DeepSeek. You sign in on that website with your own account. Whatever you send there goes directly to that service and is handled under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your password or your conversations. The website’s sign-in cookies are kept on your device.
  • Auto Reply: when you copy text, the assistant opens with that text in the message box. Nothing is sent until you tap Send yourself.

We are not affiliated with these services. Their names are used only to describe what the app works with.

PC Bridge

PC Bridge

When you pair your phone with your Windows computer, what you type is sent directly to that computer over your local Wi-Fi network or a USB cable. The connection is encrypted end to end and does not pass through our servers or any other server.

Statistics

Usage Statistics (Optional)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It is off by default and runs only if you switch it on in Page 1 → Privacy choices. You can switch it off at any time; doing so also deletes the analytics identifier on your device.

When it is on, Google collects automatically recorded usage events (such as app opens and session length), device and app details (model, Android version, language, app version), a random app-instance identifier, how the app was installed, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (not GPS). It never includes what you type, and the advertising ID is not collected for statistics.

Purpose: to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Security

App Security Check

To protect users from modified or unofficial copies, the app checks its own signature and installation source on your device. It also uses Google’s Firebase App Check with Play Integrity to confirm that the app and the device are genuine. For this check, Google receives information about the app, the device and its integrity status, under Google’s privacy policy. No text you type is involved.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app and its users safe.

Advertising

Advertising (AdMob) — Free Version Only

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and the ad service does not start until a consent decision is available. You can change your choice later in Page 1 → Privacy choices → Ad choices, where this option applies.

Google uses them to show, limit and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, only where you agree, to personalise ads. Sharing

Who Receives Data, and International Transfers

Data is sent only to the services named in this policy, and only for the purpose described for each one. We do not sell your data.

Google and several of the translation and AI services process data outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the EU and the UK, Google relies on recognised safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. The independent translation services may be located anywhere, and we cannot guarantee specific safeguards for them. This is why you should not send sensitive text to them.

Retention

How Long Data Is Kept, and How to Delete It

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear the app’s data (Android Settings → Apps → the app → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app.
  • Usage statistics (only if switched on): deleted automatically by Google Analytics after at most 14 months. Switching statistics off stops collection and deletes the identifier on your device.
  • Translation services: each service handles the text under its own terms. We do not keep a copy.
  • AI websites: your conversations are kept by the AI service in your account. To remove the sign-in from the app, sign out on the website or clear the app’s data. To delete your conversations, use that service’s own settings.
  • Advertising and security check: Google keeps this information under its own retention rules.
